# Privacy Policy

## Markdown Editor Chrome Extension

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

### Data handled by the extension
The Markdown text you enter and your language, theme, and editor-layout preferences are stored locally on your device so the editor can restore your work and settings. The extension does not send this content or these preferences to the developer or to third-party servers.

### Permissions Used
- `storage`: Used only to save your markdown content locally on your device for auto-recovery
- `clipboardWrite`: Used only when you explicitly click "Copy" buttons to copy content to your clipboard

### Data Storage
- Markdown content and language, theme, and layout preferences are stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted to any external servers
- You can clear your data by clicking the "Clear" button or uninstalling the extension
- Theme and editor-layout preferences are stored in the extension page's local storage and can be removed by clearing extension data or uninstalling the extension

### Third-Party Libraries
This extension uses the following third-party libraries:
- `marked.js` - Markdown parsing (loaded locally)
- `highlight.js` - Code syntax highlighting (loaded locally)
- `DOMPurify` - HTML sanitization (loaded locally)

These libraries are bundled with the extension and do not transmit any data.

### Sharing and analytics
The extension does not share user content with third parties and does not use analytics or advertising services. Clipboard access is used only after you click a copy button.

### Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store listing.
