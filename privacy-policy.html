Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Markdown Editor Extension

This Chrome extension ("Markdown Editor") is designed to help users write and preview Markdown content.

Data handled by the extension

The Markdown text you enter and your language, theme, and editor-layout preferences are stored locally on your device so the editor can restore your work and settings. The extension does not send this content or these preferences to the developer or to third-party servers.

Local Storage

The extension stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Auto-save your Markdown content on your device
  • Restore your work when you reopen the editor
  • Store your language and theme preferences

All editor content and preferences stay on your device. You can remove saved Markdown content with the Clear button. Other saved settings can be removed by clearing the extension's data or uninstalling it.

Permissions

  • storage: Saves your work locally (no cloud upload)
  • clipboardWrite: Copies content only when you click copy buttons

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services, and does not share user content with third parties. Clipboard access is used only after you click a copy button.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store listing.
